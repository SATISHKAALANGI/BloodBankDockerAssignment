How PulseCare Works

A simple, transparent 4-step workflow connecting voluntary life-savers with patients and healthcare institutions.

03

3. Request Blood

Our Purpose

Bridging the Gap in Critical Healthcare Logistics

PulseCare is a coordination platform designed to connect blood donors, patients, hospitals and blood banks, helping users discover blood availability and coordinate donation-related requests through a unified interface.

In medical emergencies, every second counts. Traditional manual phone requests cause critical bottlenecks. Our platform centralizes live inventory data, automates donor notification matching, and upholds transparent organ donation registries under standard health protocols.
